Mine Storm

Pilot your ship through thirteen seeded minefields.

Flight controls

Turn with A and D or the arrow keys. Hold W or ↑ to thrust. Release thrust to coast. Hold Space to fire and press X to jump to a random location. P pauses the game.

Watch where the minelayer leaves its seeds. Breaking a large or medium mine awakens two smaller mines elsewhere in the field. Magnetic mines pursue you. Fireball mines shoot back when destroyed.

You have five ships. Clear four fields to earn another. In two-player mode, pass the controls when a ship is lost.
