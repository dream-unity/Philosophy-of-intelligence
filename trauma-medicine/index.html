How Trauma Doctor Works

  • Train cold—just like a real trauma shift: Every case switches to a different clinical reasoning pattern across triage (before), resuscitation (inside), and family talks & autopsy (after). You won't see the reasoning label until the post-case debrief reveals what you just mastered.
  • Direct ward interaction: Each of the 10 bays shows a live emergency condition and its own countdown clock. Click any bay (or press keys 1–9, 0) to treat before a target reaches the redline. Right-click (or Shift+Click) a bay to rule them out.
  • Smart chart tools & cross-highlighting: Hover any bay or patient name to highlight every mention across all six charts at once. Use the toolbar to re-sort charts By Time or By Wing whenever a directive compares timestamps or wings.
  • Disputed charts (Tiers 4–6): Charts marked ? have an alternate reading. Click ? Test Flip on a chart to preview its alternate version and watch the Cross-Checks update live with ✓ / ✕ indicators until every check passes.
  • Climb the Ladder (Adaptive N-Back): Rotates cold through all 80 clinical reasoning patterns across live 3-patient crises. Pick the right patient or flag a Clinical Clash (keys 1–4) while matching Phase (A/S), Target (D/F), and Domain (H/J) against N rounds back—4 perfect rounds climb +1 Back.
