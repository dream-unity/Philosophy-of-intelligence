How Trauma Medicine Trains All 80 Reasoning Patterns

  • All 80 reasoning types, never repeating back-to-back: Cases rotate across all 8 reasoning families—Diagnostic, Logical, Relational, Quantitative, Strategic, Creative, Social/Ethical, and Metacognitive—and visit all 80 distinct patterns before repeating.
  • Three cognitive levels: Practice Atomic operations (deduction, transitivity, spatial layout), Composite coordination (differential diagnosis, Bayesian risk updates, game-theoretic triage), and Meta-Reasoning (selecting which method applies, quarantining tainted sources, and non-monotonic revision).
  • Exact action slots: Each case requires 1 to 4 patient interventions. Click a bay on the monitor or press keys 1–9, 0 before a true target reaches the redline.
  • Disputed records (Tiers 4–6): Records marked ? may have reversed arrows, swapped patients, or an alternate reading. Use the coupled Cross-Checks to determine the unique valid clinical world before acting.
