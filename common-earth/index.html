Field guide

How to play

Choose a home

Drag the globe to rotate it. Tap anywhere to survey a location, or use Explore places to search the atlas and enter exact coordinates. Found your first community when you find its home.

Plan the city

Survey the six outer precincts in City plan. Compare travel times and local impacts, then choose a project and read its cost and opening week. The central district takes you to the streets where residents live.

Fish from the waterfront

Look for Waterfront on the world atlas survey, including at Stone Town and open-water sites. In Street view, tap a moored boat or choose Board boat. Tap the water to sail or steer with the movement buttons. For a catch, commission a Fishing dock in Build and assign workers. Once it opens, board a boat and choose Cast nets. A catch costs one effort and supplies four food, once per community each week. Choose Return to shore to land.

Meet your neighbours

In Street view, open People and choose Meet beside a resident to reach their current location and open the conversation. Residents may be at home, travelling or at a worksite. You can also walk over yourself and choose Interact. Listen to what they need. Read the cost and due week before making a promise. Deliver the requested supplies, or build the project that meets the same need. Choose Spend a day together to join their work and care rounds.

Work, parcels and building

In Work, a paid shift earns 4 funds, salvage recovers 3 materials, and tending the beds produces 3 food. Each job uses one effort. For a delivery, choose Collect the market parcel, then Deliver the parcel. You travel to each stop automatically. Pickup costs one effort and handover is free, even if you have used all four effort. Delivery earns 3 funds and increases your bond with the recipient by one, up to the bond limit.

In Build, choose a community project. On a touchscreen, tap a numbered plot to see its effect, then tap the same plot again or choose Commission to pay for the work. The project opens when its crew finish building. Tap its worksite or open Build to see progress and the expected opening time. Choose Watch worksite to find the crew on the map. Use Assign on map to select residents and tap a marked worksite. You can also choose a site from the list. Under Crew and work preferences, change a resident’s occupation or let them choose. Workers travel to the job and can have other commitments. Construction runs while the game is open and visible. Dismantling takes crew time too. You can restore the facility before physical work starts. Salvage arrives when the crew clears the plot.

Plan the week

Your communities share four effort each week. Open Rehearse a decision to compare two plans before committing. Use Week preview to see what advancing would do, then Last week to review what actually happened.

Decide what endures

Listen to different voices in council. Who gains from a choice, who carries its cost, and what would you want the next generation to inherit? Choose a charter in Council. Its commitments shape what counts as success. Once the network meets them, you can hand the chair to a resident and see how they govern.

Map and street controls
Globe
Focus the map and use the arrow keys to orbit. Hold Shift to turn farther. Use + and − to zoom, Enter to survey the centre, and Home to restore the opening view. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in on a location.
Street
Tap a street to walk there. Choose Use van, then tap a road to drive there and stop. Tap another road to change your destination. Use Interact when parked near a resident or the market. The direction pad also works on touchscreens. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move, E to interact, V to board or leave the van, and Home to centre the view on your character. Select a resident in People to find their current location.
Routes and expeditions

Found a second community on the globe, then open Routes to connect your towns. They exchange supplies as the weeks pass. Independent neighbours offer bookings of their own. Check who signed and when the balance is due in Week preview.

For an expedition, select a distant destination on the atlas and choose Plan expedition here. Your choices at each stage determine the crew’s arrival time and rewards. The report shows what reached each community.

About the atlas

The globe uses Natural Earth relief, coastlines and country outlines. Zoom closer to see rivers, lakes, towns and a distance scale. Local districts and their residents are fictional. Open Chronicle → Saved worlds to start another world or export a copy of your progress.
