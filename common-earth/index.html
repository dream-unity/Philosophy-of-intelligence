    When the clock pauses

    Time pauses outside Street view, while the tab is hidden, during travel, conversation, trade, fishing, truck use, character planning, personal work and building placement. A shared day, your due promise, a council vote you reserved, a booking or a critical study decision can hold the calendar. The clock resumes when the reason clears.

    Manual advances restart the two-minute interval. Reloading this tab restarts the full interval. Founding your first community starts the clock. Its delegates can hold an unanswered council vote at the deadline. Switch to your own vote here to pause for that decision.

    Field guide

    How to play

    Choose a home

    Drag the globe to rotate it. Tap anywhere to survey a location, or use Explore places to search the atlas and enter exact coordinates. Found your first community when you find its home. To expand, open Communities and choose Found another community. Select a free location and review which community will supply the volunteers.

    Explore neighbouring streets

    Choose See streets below the atlas, or open Towns in Street view. Follow a resident at work. Your character stays where they are until you choose to travel. At home, open Workplaces → Neighbourhood projects to review a proposal for a particular precinct.

    Fish from the waterfront

    Open Workplaces → Waterfront on the street map. For shore fishing, choose Go shore fishing to walk to the quay, then Cast line. When the float dips, choose Reel in to bring one food to the pantry. A bite waits for you. For ocean fishing, choose Board a boat, then Follow seabirds, cast and reel in for two food. The shoal moves after you harvest it. For a larger weekly haul, build a Fishing dock and use Cast nets aboard a boat for four food when the crew is ready.

    Meet your neighbours

    Open People and choose Meet beside a name to walk over. You can also walk up and choose Interact. Questions become available when you are together. The reply stays in the conversation, with a speech bubble beside the person. Residents may be at home, travelling or at work. Listen to what they need. Read the cost and due week before making a promise. Deliver the requested supplies, or build the project that meets the same need. Choose Spend a day together to join their work and care rounds.

    Personal work and the handcart

    Open Personal work in Street view for food, funds or materials. These jobs use seconds of time. They are available even when the community has spent all four effort. Walk to the named supplier for a quicker, larger load, or return to the market after the timer finishes. Another resident can collect the job you started.

    Build a community handcart with two materials to carry larger loads. Anyone can use it. Fit produce baskets for a bigger food harvest or a timber rack for more materials. Find the finished cart beside the market exchange.

    Character lives and changing careers

    To switch characters, choose their name under Play as beneath the street map. In expanded view, open Town goals, choose the person and tap Play as. You take over at their current location. To follow their work while someone else stays under your control, choose See. Open Character lives to explore a person’s development. Abilities shows what comes easily and how far practice can take a skill. In Work & study, compare a career’s requirements with that person’s ability, train over several weeks, then apply. A hobby remains worth practising even when a profession’s entry standard is out of reach.

    Make choices for the person you play. Visit someone else in person to propose a course or discuss their plans. Week by week records their own priorities and decisions. Use the calendar to preview the effect of advancing.

    Work, parcels and building

    For personal wages, open Resources, visit the exchange, take a paid stock check, ask the named resident for the count and return to collect. You can keep your pay or pool it with neighbours to buy supplies. The worker’s earnings and town treasury are separate.

    In Work, a community shift earns 4 funds, salvage recovers 3 materials, and tending the beds produces 3 food. Each completed job also pays 1 personal fund, earns 1 development point and restores 1 civic effort up to four. For a delivery, choose Collect the market parcel, then Deliver the parcel. You travel to each stop automatically. You can collect parcels at zero effort. The next parcel opens after handover. Delivery earns 3 funds and increases your bond with the recipient by one, up to the bond limit.

    In Build, choose a community project. Use Pin plan to keep its supply list in view across Street tabs and visits. Compare lots to see how neighbouring buildings affect the weekly yield, and mark a preferred spot while you earn the supplies. Marking a lot is free. You pay when you commission the building. On a touchscreen, tap a numbered plot to see its effect, then tap the same plot again or choose Commission to pay for the work. The project opens when its crew finish building. Tap its worksite or open Build to see progress and the expected opening time. Choose See worksite to find the crew on the map. Use Assign on map to select residents and tap a marked worksite. You can also choose a site from the list. Under Crew and weekly duties, ask a resident to help with a community duty or let them choose. Their career appears in Character lives. Workers travel to the job and can have other commitments. Construction runs while the game is open and visible. Dismantling takes crew time too. You can restore the facility before physical work starts. Salvage arrives when the crew clears the plot.

    Plan the week

    Founding your first community starts Live weeks. Open Calendar on the street map to pause or resume the calendar. One week passes after two minutes of visible Street time. Conversations, trips and decisions that need you pause the clock. Your communities share four civic effort each week for organised actions. Personal jobs, ordinary conversations and handline fishing can continue after those four points are used. Open Rehearse a decision to compare two plans before committing. Use Week preview to see what advancing would do, then Last week to review what actually happened.

    Decide what endures

    Listen to different voices in council. Who gains from a choice, who carries its cost, and what would you want the next generation to inherit? Choose a charter in Council. Its commitments shape what counts as success. Once the network meets them, you can hand the chair to a resident and see how they govern.

    Map and street controls
    Globe
    Focus the map and use the arrow keys to orbit. Hold Shift to turn farther. Use + and − to zoom, Enter to survey the centre, and Home to restore the opening view. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in on a location.
    Street
    Tap a street to walk there. Choose Use public van, then tap a road to drive there and stop. Tap another road to change your destination. Step out of the van to talk face to face. Tap a park bench or choose Find a seat to walk there and sit. Move or choose Stand up to leave. Tap a noticeboard in the park or beside the market to read local news. Use Calendar to let the whole calendar advance after two minutes of visible Street time. It pauses during hands-on work and for decisions that need your attention. The direction pad also works on touchscreens. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move, E to interact, V to board or leave the van, and Home to centre the view on your character. Open People and choose Meet to walk to a named neighbour and speak face to face. Tap Town goals for community needs, or My aim for your own projects. Use the person selector to see a neighbour at work. Resources shows supplies, orders, and the exchange. Waterfront districts also have a fishing station under Workplaces.
    Follow a resident’s life

    Open Town goals on the street, then People and their goals. Pick a name to see what the person wants, what they depend on and who will feel the effect. Choose See to follow their route and Meet to walk over. Their background and strengths appear under About.

    A career change takes time. Training, a sample of work, an employer’s decision and a handover can each hold up the move. A resident can decline your suggestion. If you take another person’s viewpoint, the Original life button keeps your own agreements and home pantry round within reach.

    Observe the region

    Drag the street past the last building to explore the land between communities. Pinch to see farther. Tap a point on the bottom-left minimap to inspect it, or open Atlas and choose whose exploration to show. The original resident stays where they are. Use Development ledger to follow local work, the people carrying it out and changes to their purses.

    Learn a skill or commission work

    Open Calendar → Practice and decisions. Choose an activity, an approach and what you expect to happen. Meet your partner or walk to the work location. Afterward, Decision history keeps the first attempt, any adjustment and the result. When someone else took part, select their name to read their account.

    Visit a neighbouring community

    Open Towns on the street and choose a destination. Walk to the exchange to set out. Along the trail you can inspect landmarks, choose a fork or return home. At the destination, meet the named host and check the admission requirements. A trial shift, housing, meals and the local council’s agreement can determine whether you can stay. People you invite to follow make their own decisions.

    Choose what to gather and keep

    Survey a site on the globe to see its food sources and materials. On the street, Resources distinguishes what can be gathered locally, what the public bins hold and what a merchant will sell. Check the season, remaining stock and storage date before choosing between gathering, processing or trade. At a fishing district, open Workplaces → Waterfront, walk to the marked spot and cast. Reel in after a bite to bring the catch to the pantry.

    Choose Exit truck on the street map to park and step out. Re-enter truck lets you take the wheel again at the same kerb. Tap the blue co-op truck to see its owner, current pickup and custody. A work assignment or permission gives access to the keys. Taking it without permission can delay a delivery immediately. Ask residents what they saw. Dispatch can identify the operator when a witness or an account reaches it.

    Routes and expeditions

    Found a second community on the globe, then open Routes to connect your towns. They exchange supplies as the weeks pass. Independent neighbours offer bookings of their own. Check who signed and when the balance is due in Week preview.

    For an expedition, select a distant destination on the atlas and choose Plan expedition here. Your choices at each stage determine the crew’s arrival time and rewards. The report shows what reached each community.

    About the atlas

    The globe uses Natural Earth relief, coastlines and country outlines. Zoom closer to see rivers, lakes, towns and a distance scale. Local districts and their residents are fictional. Open Chronicle → Saved worlds to start another world or export a copy of your progress.
