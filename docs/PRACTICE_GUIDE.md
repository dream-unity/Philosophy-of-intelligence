# A practical path through Philosophy of Intelligence

Begin with Circle. One shape may occupy several visits. Repeat the cycle below, moving forward when you can explain the operation and correct it using feedback. Every shape remains available for exploration.

## Repeat this cycle for each shape

1. **Orient.** Open the lesson and inspect its worked example. Say the operation in ordinary words: “I am matching roles across two situations.” Identify what the supplied information allows you to conclude.

2. **Complete eight Guided challenges.** Use hints or source reveals to understand the operation. After an error, identify what failed: the feature, earlier record, direction, role, rule or boundary. Inspect the construction diagram where available. Save a useful principle to the Palace afterwards.

3. **Revisit.** On a later visit, explain that principle before reopening the example. Complete another Guided mission if the operation feels uncertain. Change one demand at a time; a visit can focus entirely on learning.

4. **Try twenty Independent challenges.** Do this when you can attempt the operation without the example beside you. Read both the overall result and the component channels. Help remains available, but using it makes that attempt assisted. Pause if needed; there is no response deadline. Choose your next practice from the error pattern.

5. **Make a small bridge.** Open Breath-back at the same shape. Record one ordinary observation and follow the prompt. Describe a relationship when asked; “no clear relation” is useful. Keep the original and add later interpretations as notes.

Your first visit can end after one Guided mission; Independent checks and personal bridges can wait.

## What each new shape adds

| Shape | New operation | A question to carry into practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circle | Notice one relevant feature. | Which detail answers this question? |
| Half-circle | Bind an item to its location. | What was there, and where? |
| Triangle | Combine relations into a directed inference. | What follows from both stated facts? |
| Square | Retrieve an exact earlier observation. | Is this the right occurrence to compare? |
| Pentagon | Use the stated context. | Which condition changes the comparison? |
| Hexagon | Organise dependencies. | What must happen before what? |
| Triangular prism | Map three corresponding roles. | What performs the same role elsewhere? |
| Heptagon | Derive and apply a function. | What rule fits these cases and predicts another? |
| Octagon | Transfer a function with its boundaries. | Do the roles correspond, and are its conditions satisfied here? |

| Geodesic sphere | Diagnose and repair an operation. | Which particular step needs changing? |
| Sphere | Revise a model using new evidence. | What changes, and what remains supported? |

## Adjust difficulty deliberately

In **Square → Guided**, start at **1-back** with one lens: colour, shape or value. N counts **committed observations**, not breaths or clicks. The first N records prepare the history and are unscored. Retrieve the exact earlier record before judging its feature.

Try Mixed at the same lag before increasing N. Independent Square uses mixed features. If you lose the earlier occurrence, lower the lag; if you retrieve it correctly but misapply the comparison, return to a fixed lens. Change settings between missions. Greater retrieval distance and greater relational complexity are separate demands.

## Let breathing support your pace

The guide is optional. Its editable starting point is four seconds in and four seconds out, without holds; each phase allows two to eight seconds. Use a comfortable, unforced rhythm or leave it off. One action may take several breaths. The guide never advances or scores the task. Stop it if uncomfortable.

## Return to evidence

When working on functions, try the model lab under **The philosophy**: commit a prediction before revealing the output, then inspect whether the test was within the reference conditions. Lab experiments clear on reset or reload.

From **Heptagon** onward, the studio can preserve a situation, prediction and revision trigger. Later, open the Palace entry and add what happened. Distinguish the observation from your interpretation; “still unclear” is available.

For Philosophy of Intelligence's independent mastery marker, meet the criteria on **two distinct UTC dates** at the same lag and unit: twenty unaided independent attempts from completed sessions per date, 85% overall and in every required channel, and 90% nonmatch accuracy where applicable. Square also needs five match and five nonmatch trials per date. Distinct dates need not be 24 hours apart. Continue exploring while building that evidence.
