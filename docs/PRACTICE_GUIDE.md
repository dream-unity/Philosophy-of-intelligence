# Practising relationships through the shapes

Choose a shape and Consciousness Stream, Heart-Coherence or Body-Sensation Mapping. Read the theory and four worked cycles. All fourteen shapes remain available.

## One complete return

1. **Inhale: receive and choose** the meaningful relationship or relational model actually available.
2. **Exhale: encode first** at a distinct address in your imagined home or another palace.
3. **Then retrieve** the exact earlier entry required by the chosen lag.
4. **Compare and annotate**, keeping both sources unchanged and uncertainty explicit.

The first entry seeds history when there is no earlier target. Every shape uses this complete procedure.

At Circle, ocean and boat can remain in the same imagined scene. On the first inhale, select **ocean contains boat**; encode that one relationship at the doorway on the exhale. On the next inhale, select **boat enables passage across ocean**; encode it at the hallway shelf before retrieving the doorway entry. Compare: the participants remain, but the selected meaning changes from containment to traversal function. The first relationship stays in its original record and may remain true. Two participants do not turn Circle into a two-relationship task.

## Reading the four-cycle examples

Example contents are demonstrations, not images or sensations you must produce. Deliberate constructions are labelled rehearsal material. In a compact one-entry-per-breath sequence, each return after the first entry addresses the immediately preceding entry, after securing the current source.

Larger examples require a prepared, recoverable model. Inspect its register and learn it over as many ordinary breaths as needed. A model's name is not a substitute for its thirty or 120 links. The short trace demonstrates working with that prepared structure; it does not impose a four-breath learning deadline. If a record takes several breaths, use record-paced returns and describe the actual work honestly.

## Three settings

Consciousness Stream uses inner images, words, remembered sounds and other selected contents. It starts in your own rhythm with sound off. Heart-Coherence retains heart-focused attention, heart feeling and deepening. Body-Sensation Mapping repeats location, quality, intensity, movement, direction, coherence and meaning-tone, with 30 seconds per dimension.

Heart practice initially offers five seconds in and five seconds out, with distinct bells after Begin. Mute the bell, choose your own rhythm, pause or end as needed. Body begins naturally and silently. The guide does not detect breathing, measure a heart state or guarantee a feeling.

## A written return

The form's N counts committed observations in the same shape, exercise and stream. Choose one to four entries back. This is not an automatic breath-indexed return.

Write the current source first. When history exists, **Encode current entry, then retrieve** locks the source, lag and exact target. Record your recollection before revealing the original; “I do not recall” is valid. Reveal locks the recollection and enables comparison and correction. Commit the complete entry to save it. A draft lock alone is not a save.

Accurately recalling that an earlier source was uncertain differs from failing to retrieve it. Preserve that distinction. A fresh stream resets working history while retaining saved entries.

## Keeping your work

Readiness is your reversible assessment for each setting, without gating. The practice-record download includes committed private notes and assessments, not drafts or active timers. Drafts survive navigation within the open page, not a reload.

The record permits 500 observations and 500 practice periods, with 3,000 characters per text field. At the practice-period limit, starting another recorded timer is blocked; untimed practice remains possible. Export before explicitly clearing it at capacity. There is no individual observation deletion or current-practice import. Older heart/body data and the separate Archive remain available. If persistence fails or a conflict occurs, export the work in the current tab before leaving. If loading a saved version removes a locked return's exact target, use the separate unfinished-return recovery download before beginning new history with the same current source; it preserves the original target snapshot and unfinished fields.
