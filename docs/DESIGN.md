# Philosophy of Intelligence: design rationale

Philosophy of Intelligence asks how a person can preserve what they encounter, organise relationships within it, and turn a useful pattern into something they can apply and revise. Its geometric progression is a practical interpretation of a philosophy of intelligence, expressed through exercises rather than a test of a person's worth or potential.

## Geometry as a language for operations

The initial philosophical intuition connects shapes with the capacity to hold relationships together. Philosophy of Intelligence uses this as a teaching language. A form gives an operation a memorable identity; its visual structure can suggest connection, coordination or an organised whole.

That choice does not make a polygon's side count a measurement of cognitive capacity. A circle is not being asserted to be a conventional one-sided polygon. A three-dimensional solid is not evidence that its user has reached a higher biological kind of intelligence. The fourteen **70–200 labels are symbolic markers** attached to stages, not measured or estimated IQ.

The curriculum separates three questions:

1. **What is represented?** A selected feature, item, episode, role, relation, rule or model.
2. **What operation is performed?** Binding, connecting, mapping, applying, testing or revising.
3. **What makes the result accountable?** A source, a declared comparison, an answer condition or a possible observation that could change it.

Holding more material and organising material differently are distinct demands. The design foregrounds one new operation at a time, reuses prior operations, and makes the relevant material explicit. Later tasks do not become harder merely by hiding more information or imposing a shorter deadline.

## A progression with a reason for each step

| Stage | New emphasis | Why it belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circle — Notice | Select a relevant feature. | Begin with a small, visible source before adding retrieval. |
| Half-circle — Bind | Preserve an item-location pair. | A remembered feature needs an identifiable source. |
| Triangle — Connect | Integrate stated relationships. | Move from separate facts to an inference supported by their structure. |
| Square — Return | Retrieve an exact earlier episode. | Distinguish familiarity from correct temporal binding. |
| Pentagon — Contextualise | Apply a context-dependent comparison. | The same material can require a different judgment under another stated rule. |
| Hexagon — Organise | Reconstruct dependencies. | Individual relationships can constrain a whole sequence. |
| Triangular prism — Map | Preserve three roles across domains. | Transfer depends on corresponding functions and relationships. |
| Heptagon — Abstract | Derive and use a rule. | A pattern becomes a procedure that supports a prediction. |
| Octagon — Transfer | Carry a function into a new domain and check applicability. | A transferred rule must preserve corresponding roles and the conditions under which its prediction is supported. |
| Cube — Coordinate | Align four roles across accounts. | An additional role adds a coordination demand while retaining the mapping operation. |
| Dodecahedron — Select | Choose from a small function library. | Knowing a rule and retrieving the relevant rule are different tasks. |
| Icosahedron — Discriminate | Choose a diagnostic test. | Competing explanations need evidence that can distinguish them. |
| Geodesic sphere — Repair | Diagnose a process error. | Correction should address the failed operation rather than merely repeat an answer. |
| Sphere — Revise | Coordinate function, prediction and update. | A model remains useful when its status can change with evidence. |

This sequence is a designed apprenticeship, not an empirically established developmental ladder. Every stage remains accessible. Returning to an earlier stage supports a particular operation; it does not downgrade the person.

## Teach the operation before measuring independent performance

Each stage supplies a concise lesson, steps, a worked example and fresh authored challenges. Guided practice allows help. Independent practice preserves whether help was used, so revealing the source or hint cannot later be counted as an unaided answer.

Missions contain eight or twenty scored challenges. Short missions offer a manageable learning unit. Longer missions can contribute sufficient observations for an independent check. Neither length is presented as a proven training dose. Both modes permit either length, and several completed missions can contribute to the same date's independent evidence.

Feedback explains the source-supported operation. A connection must preserve its declared direction; an ordering must preserve dependencies; a mapping must be consistent across its roles; a function must respect its stated conditions. An appealing explanation alone is not the scoring rule.

Connection, ordering and mapping tasks also draw the learner's construction. The diagram provides an inspectable record of the proposed relationships. Feedback can identify an error without replacing the original proposal with a diagram that makes it appear correct. This gives the geometric metaphor a practical role beyond stage decoration.

Settings are fixed during a mission. A submitted attempt cannot be submitted again for extra credit. Pausing preserves the task, and an interrupted mission is restored in a paused state. No response deadline is introduced by the breathing guide or by the lesson sequence.

## Keep difficulty dimensions separate

Philosophy of Intelligence distinguishes retrieval distance from relational complexity. Square's N-back setting counts **committed observations**, not seconds, breaths or the number of interface interactions. The first N records establish history and are unscored. A later comparison refers to the observation exactly N positions earlier.

Retrieving the source and comparing its feature are separate responses. A familiar item from the wrong episode must not receive binding credit simply because its feature happens to yield the right comparison. Increasing N changes the retrieval demand; it does not independently demonstrate more sophisticated relational understanding.

Guided Square practice can hold the comparison lens to colour, shape or value before introducing mixed-feature questions. Independent Square practice uses mixed features. This lets a beginner learn exact temporal binding without also having to adapt to a changing comparison dimension on every trial. The selected lens is frozen for the mission.

Advanced stages can provide visible sources while asking for a richer operation. They are not all described as N-back tasks. The private studio can use earlier committed observations while retaining its own unscored exploration purpose.

## Assessment is specific and inspectable

Whole-challenge correctness and component channels serve different purposes. A whole challenge succeeds only when its required responses are correct. Channel scores expose where the operation succeeded or failed. The current channels are observation, binding, relation, mapping, function and revision.

For example, Square requires both binding and relation; Prism and Cube assess mapping; Sphere requires mapping, function and revision. This prevents a strong score in one component from concealing an absent or weak required component.

The current progression rules are:

- **Practice completed:** a completed session with at least six qualifying attempts and at least 75% whole-challenge accuracy.
- **One independent check:** at least twenty eligible unaided independent attempts from completed sessions on one recorded date, at least 85% whole-challenge accuracy, and at least 85% in each required channel.
- **Independent mastery:** the independent criteria hold on two distinct recorded dates at the same lag and lag unit.
- **Answer-class checks:** nonmatch accuracy reaches at least 90% wherever nonmatch trials occur. Square also requires at least five match and five nonmatch trials on each qualifying date.

Dates are currently grouped by the UTC date in the attempt timestamp. Two distinct dates are not a claim of a minimum 24-hour separation. Assisted attempts, tutorials and warmups do not contribute to independent mastery. Sessions must be completed and their attempt metadata must agree with the session record.

The class checks address a simple failure of raw accuracy: always selecting the common answer can appear successful on an unbalanced task. Requiring evidence from both classes and checking nonmatches separately makes that shortcut less useful. These checks do not convert the game into a standardised assessment.

The thresholds are explicit product rules. There is no claim that 85%, twenty attempts, or two dates proves a lasting psychological ability. A stage badge records a bounded achievement on these exercises and under these conditions.

## Preserve sources and append revisions

Learning from an error requires access to what was originally presented and answered. Each generated challenge has a stable source and identity. Repeated wording or values do not make two occurrences the same episode. The session records the committed response before showing feedback.

The same principle applies to private exploration. The original observation stays intact. A later interpretation or correction is a revision attached to that record, rather than an overwrite that makes the original appear more accurate than it was. A source, a remembered representation and a proposed interpretation have different roles.

Generated tasks have answers grounded in their supplied material. Private reflections do not inherit that certainty. The app should not infer that a journal entry establishes another person's motives, verifies a memory, or becomes more factual through repetition. An unresolved connection may remain unresolved.

## The bridge to free breath-back

The studio makes the taught operations available within private experience: notice something, select a representation, place it at a locus, revisit a specified earlier observation, and name a relationship or uncertainty. Later stages invite a proposed function and its limits.

A function is broadly understood as an applicable transformation or procedure. It can be an equation, an if–then rule or a short sequence of actions. A useful record states when it applies, what it predicts and what would count against it. This gives an analogy a possible use while preserving the distinction between a proposal and a demonstrated result.

The authored curriculum and the studio are compatible because they share operations and source discipline. Their assessment remains different. The app can objectively score its supplied tasks, while private reflection remains unscored. Writing more, repeating a phrase or producing a persuasive personal narrative does not earn mastery.

At the function stages, an optional prediction record separates three commitments: the situation in which the pattern might apply, the expected observation, and a result that would prompt revision. A later Palace visit appends the reported outcome, the user's judgment of its bearing on the prediction, and any revision. The initial prediction remains visible. “Still unclear” is a first-class outcome; the application does not certify the user's interpretation.

Memory-palace locations serve as retrieval cues. They do not imply a complete recording of experience. Words, simple spatial cues and other representations remain available without requiring vivid mental imagery. The Palace stores selected principles and observations so that they can be revisited and revised.

## Four philosophical lenses in one small experiment

The model lab gives strength, density, parameters and horizon points working meanings through an authored signal machine. Initially, green `2 → 4` is compatible with both “add two” and “double.” The given cases `3 → 5` and `5 → 7` distinguish these candidates without proving that either is a universal law.

The learner chooses a model, input and context, then commits a prediction before revealing an outcome. The committed settings cannot be edited afterwards. New experiments preserve earlier predictions and observations, making revision visible rather than rewriting the history of the model.

| Lens | What the interaction makes inspectable |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | How the candidate fares against distinct observations inside the reference region, including conflicting evidence. Repeating a deterministic case does not increase the number of distinct conditions tested. |
| Density | How input, context, rule and predicted output form a connected account. More decorative links are not assigned greater intelligence. |
| Parameters | Which inputs or contextual settings can vary while the other conditions stay fixed. |
| Horizon | Where the supplied reference ends and applying the candidate becomes an extrapolation. |

The reference region is green inputs 0–6. The authored machine adds two there; amber or overflow inputs use a route that doubles. A failed extrapolation does not refute the original model inside its stated conditions. Conversely, an agreeing extrapolation supplies one new case rather than permission to erase every boundary. The full simulator definition can be inspected separately from the inferential exercise.

This lab has no scores, mastery contribution or persistent storage. It retains its experimental history while mounted and clears on reset or reload. Its evidence is synthetic. It supplies a compact environment for inspecting the philosophy, not a demonstration of transfer or a test of intelligence.

## A rhythm that leaves the learner in control

Breathing is an optional attention cue. The editable starting setting is four seconds inhaling and four seconds exhaling, with no hold. Each phase can be adjusted from two to eight seconds; free breathing remains available. Sound is off by default and uses optional soft phase tones.

The task waits for the learner. One observation or response may take several cycles. The guide never advances a trial, grades compliance, detects a breath or estimates a physiological state. Pausing the guide stops its tones, and hiding the page pauses it. Reduced-motion support preserves phase information without requiring continuous visual movement.

The design asks for comfortable, unforced breathing and allows the guide to be stopped. Its timing is not a medical prescription. The comfort principles and their source are discussed in [Reference notes](REFERENCE_NOTES.md); they do not establish an efficacy claim for Philosophy of Intelligence.

## Local ownership and deliberate export

Philosophy of Intelligence runs without a backend. Browser local storage holds preferences, attempts, completed sessions, Palace entries and private observations. No analytics, external AI calls or automatic uploads are part of the application. The public repository contains the application and source commentary, not the private attachments that informed its development.

The default JSON export removes private journal content, private Palace entries and the active session. A deliberate choice is required to include private content. Compact JSON keeps Philosophy of Intelligence's own valid exports re-importable without shortening their included private text. Unsaved form drafts and the transient model lab are outside the export.

The storage limits distinguish private records from rolling practice evidence:

| Material | Limit and behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Palace and journal together | 8 MiB of compact UTF-8 JSON, at most 128 Palace entries and 100 journal entries. A write that exceeds a limit is refused; the draft remains in the form. |
| Attempt log | The newest records within both 3,000 attempts and 4 MiB. |
| Session log | The newest records within both 1,000 sessions and 4 MiB. |
| Import file | Up to 32 MiB, with the same collection, field and record validation. |

Private text is not silently shortened to satisfy a size limit. Oversized private imports are rejected so the original file can be retained. At capacity, the user can export committed records and remove an older entry before retrying; an unsaved draft must be copied separately before leaving. Private text fields generally allow 3,000 characters, with up to twenty later notes and twenty evidence records per entry. The larger import allowance accommodates a full private collection, both bounded logs and the unfinished session.

Rolling logs are intentionally finite. Stage summaries are calculated from retained evidence, so older practice or mastery evidence can eventually leave the current summary. Exports preserve longer histories outside the app; imports do not bypass the rolling limits.

Local storage is convenient persistence, not encryption or guaranteed archival storage. A browser's quota can be smaller than the application's budgets. Clearing, profile changes or disabled storage can also affect it. Save failures produce a warning that the latest work is only in the open tab and should be exported before leaving.

Another tab's save triggers a conflict banner; the older tab pauses its mission and breath guide and refuses further writes. A check immediately before saving also compares the stored snapshot. The user can export the older tab's committed records, then reload the saved version. There is no automatic merge or cross-device synchronisation, and unsaved form drafts must be copied before reloading.

Imports are checked for supported schema and bounded content. Imported text is rendered as text rather than executable markup. Local progress is still a personal practice record, not a tamper-proof credential.

## Technical form follows the learning design

Plain scripts separate curriculum, session state, progress, geometry, breathing, the model lab and interface behaviour. The same core modules run in the browser and in Node's test runner. The app has no runtime packages and uses local SVG artwork, system fonts and browser-generated tones.

The offline build packages the experience in one HTML file. A static source deployment is also possible. Neither deployment mode changes the meaning of the scores or introduces account synchronisation.

The implementation contract records module responsibilities and invariants. Tests should protect substantive behaviours: exact-lag retrieval, stable sources, correct mapping evaluation, permanent aid status, class-aware mastery, privacy-preserving export and an independent breathing clock. They cannot establish educational or clinical efficacy.

## What would need separate evidence

The project implements a coherent proposal for practice. It does not establish that the shape sequence describes the structure of human intelligence, that the symbolic labels correspond to IQ, or that practice transfers to everyday reasoning, wellbeing or clinical outcomes.

Those questions would require appropriate measurements, comparison conditions and follow-up beyond successful play. The immediate design commitment is narrower and inspectable: make each operation understandable, preserve its source, expose errors, and give the learner a way to correct them.

## The engraved visual language

The interface uses warm parchment, graphite-coloured line work, serif type and orbital construction diagrams. The home plate makes Observe, Relate and Revise into working links; the three framed pathways lead to lessons, breath-back and the Palace. Lessons, exercises, private records, dialogs and the model lab share this visual language. The visible identity is Philosophy of Intelligence.

Vector engravings use deterministic fine traces and restrained face hatching. Decorative texture and construction lines do not replace labels or task data. Feature-colour swatches remain coloured when colour is part of the exercise; sources also name those colours in text. Reduced motion keeps the artwork centred and removes its breathing movement. All artwork, textures and typography work without network fonts or image requests.
