# Reference notes and boundaries

## Controlling project method

The supplied **Philosophy of Intelligence Consciousness Streaming Manual**, revised working edition **2.2**, provides the background framework. The user's latest conceptual-relationship clarifications control where earlier definitions differ: Circle represents **one explicit meaningful relationship**, such as ocean contains boat, not an isolated whole or a symbolic self-loop. Half-circle holds **two distinct meanings**, such as ocean contains boat and boat enables passage across ocean. Two participants do not imply two relationships. Inward receiving/choosing precedes outward encoding, which precedes deliberate historical retrieval and comparison. Palace placement and return are present at every shape.

Later graph assignments are declared working conventions, not established cognitive-science classifications. Dodecahedron and Icosahedron both have thirty selected links. The specified Geodesic sphere has 42 roles and 120 links; Sphere uses a declared 120-link integrated model in these examples. Neither name implies a universal count.

The supplied monograph **Eating the Ego: An Ouroboros of Becoming** provides background for consciousness streaming, palace encoding, return and revision. The precise interface order and shape-count rules are implementation choices informed by the clarified method, not externally validated findings. The earlier property-based, whole-only and procedure-unlocking lessons are superseded. The new examples foreground conceptual meanings such as function, dependence and constraint; incidental attributes do not define their counts.

## Retained exercise source

The source is [Project Meaning](https://o0ooooooooooo0o.github.io/Project-Meaning/) and its [repository at commit fb0553e87b840775438fa3107490b0085b067192](https://github.com/o0oooooOooooo0o/Project-Meaning/commit/fb0553e87b840775438fa3107490b0085b067192), as recorded in the existing implementation. The application does not fetch that site at runtime.

Retained mechanics include three heart phases, heart durations of 15/30/60/120/180 minutes, seven repeating body dimensions at 30 seconds each, body durations of 15/30/60 minutes, synthesized bowls, completion cues and ambient pulse options. Five-second heart phase cues are optional. Their timing is not a physiological measurement or a universally optimal prescription.

Consciousness Stream, the relational teaching, four-cycle examples and ordered note capture are this project's additions. Heart/body sources supply exercise contexts; they do not establish the shape progression or its efficacy.

## Interpretation and evidence

An inner experience can be faithfully recorded without establishing an external fact. Mark constructions, uncertainty and inference. Spatial position does not identify an emotion; temporal succession does not establish causation. Comparison cannot establish information absent from the original source.

Readiness is the learner's judgement. Time spent, completing a model or increasing its link count does not establish a health outcome, intelligence gain or transfer beyond the task. The application has no physiological sensor and performs no clinical assessment.

Four-cycle examples are teaching traces. Prepared larger structures must remain recoverable through their registers; they are not beginner achievements promised within four breaths. The stored lag counts committed observations. Describing it as a measured breath lag without an actual breath record would be inaccurate.
