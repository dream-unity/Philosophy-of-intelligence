# Reference notes and design boundaries

FORM develops a geometric apprenticeship in relational reasoning. Its identity comes from a progression of operations: observe, preserve, relate, compare, map, model, test and revise. The supplied reference documents and earlier trainer informed this design; they are not redistributed in this public repository.

## Influences

### The geometric philosophy

The proposed progression runs from circle through half-circle, triangle, square, pentagon, hexagon, triangular prism, heptagon, octagon, cube, dodecahedron, icosahedron and geodesic sphere to sphere. Each form names a training stage. The associated 70–200 labels, in steps of ten, are invented philosophical symbols. They are not measured IQ, estimated IQ, entry requirements, intelligence classifications or promised gains. Progress records describe performance on the game's particular exercises.

The forms distinguish growth in the number of relevant aspects from growth in how relationships and systems are organised. The curriculum gives those ideas concrete exercises; geometric appearance alone does not establish a cognitive hierarchy.

### *Eating the Ego: An Ouroboros of Becoming*

The supplied monograph describes a loop of observation, felt care, selective encoding, comparison with an earlier experience, and further encoding of the relationship discovered. The observing or judging stance can itself become material for examination. This is a conceptual and philosophical influence, not evidence of the game's efficacy.

Specific design influences include:

- A memory-palace location is a cue for returning to a selected representation, not a complete recording of experience (page 12).
- A compressed representation should preserve enough detail to be reopened and corrected (page 23).
- A comparison needs a declared dimension; a repeated image can coexist with a changed relationship to that image (page 26).
- An unresolved relationship can remain unresolved (page 29).
- Imagined, recalled, inferred and externally reported material have different provenance (pages 30 and 44).
- Further reflection should have a practical stopping condition (page 32).
- An encoding or retrieval mistake should lead to a specific repair rather than a judgment about the person (page 59).
- Vivid visual imagery is not a prerequisite; words, spatial cues and other representations can be useful (page 75).
- A proposed interpretation can be tested through a proportionate action and its actual consequences (page 103).

The new beginner progression is a design addition. It does not infer a universal practice duration or a clinical result from the monograph.

### *Shared-Content Relational N-Back*

The supplied theoretical paper distinguishes an episode's source, selected pattern, numerical tag, relational representation, cross-domain mapping and evaluation. Its main contribution to this project is **accountability between representations of the same source**. It reports a theory and an experimental programme, not new participant results establishing this game's benefits.

The paper separates numerical identity, structural equivalence, episodic binding, mapping adequacy and confidence calibration. An accurate numerical answer cannot certify that the associated experience was remembered; a fluent analogy cannot certify that it preserves its source. See its formal specification in section 5 and operational protocol in section 10.

### *Sentience N-Back v1.9 Offline*

The supplied single-file application provides working examples of exact-lag number training and relational-pattern comparison, along with embedded offline audio. Its relational mode matches equal-status premises using one consistent mapping across the entire trial. Reordering premises and reversing a relation with its appropriate inverse can preserve structure. This is different from testing whether a premise follows from the other premises.

Useful interaction principles include unscored initial history, frozen session settings, separate task scores, explicit explanations, pause and resume, and an unscored listening mode. Its optional synthesis and mathematical-meta exercises are described for private practice; that application does not capture or objectively assess those personal constructions. The presence of numerical prefixes and suffixes alone does not implement the paper's shared-source architecture.

FORM introduces a new curriculum and interaction model instead of reproducing the earlier trainer's settings screen and compass-premise game. Its authored exercises can teach source selection, role preservation, model consequences and justified revision. Private exploration is a separate route with different assessment boundaries.

## Shared-content and source commitments

1. **A source is fixed for the attempt.** Preserve the presented source and the representation initially committed by the learner. A correction is a separate revision, with a reason, rather than an edit that makes the original appear correct.
2. **An episode has an identity and a position.** Repeated digits, wording or content do not make two occurrences the same episode. Binding checks should distinguish the intended source from a familiar source at the wrong lag.
3. **N counts the declared unit.** In trial-based N-back, the current trial compares with the trial exactly N positions earlier. The first N trials build history and do not create scored comparisons. A breathing cue is not silently substituted for a trial boundary.
4. **Define the comparison.** A task must state whether it concerns an exact feature, a source identity, a role mapping, a dependency, a consequence or a structural recurrence. One answer cannot silently stand in for another.
5. **Preserve roles across the whole mapping.** A learner cannot choose incompatible mappings independently for different edges. If more than one complete mapping is genuinely valid, accept the valid alternatives or author an unambiguous item.
6. **Make correction inspectable.** Feedback should identify what was retained, changed, omitted or unsupported. Revealing the earlier source after an unaided answer can make the correction concrete.
7. **Distinguish observation from interpretation.** A personal scene or explanation may be useful while remaining uncertain. Repeated attention does not supply independent corroboration.

## Scoring and progression

Authored exercises have answers determined by their supplied material. Their channels can be assessed independently. A source-binding mistake must remain visible even when another component is correct. Assisted attempts, worked examples, warmups and independent assessment are distinct conditions.

Private journal material, associations and introspective models are not automatically assigned factual correctness. The game can support recording, source marking, comparison and self-review without pretending to determine another person's motives or the truth of an autobiographical interpretation. Word overlap and a persuasive explanation are not a correctness standard.

Mastery must require enough independent observations and evidence on the relevant channels. Raw accuracy can be misleading with uneven answer classes. For example, the reference trainer's three-premise, 50% whole-trial-match configuration permits an always-Yes strategy to average about 83% per-premise accuracy. Independent uniform digits from one to nine permit an always-No strategy to average about 89%. These are reasons to inspect both match and nonmatch performance and to use diagnostic probes, not thresholds borrowed from another task.

Increasing temporal lag, represented material, relational order, time pressure and reduced assistance are separate design choices. A good progression can change one demand while preserving the others. Returning to an easier supported exercise is a learning operation, not a reduction in the person's intelligence.

## Breathing and attention

The optional breathing guide provides a timing or attention cue. It does not measure breathing, detect physiological states, grade bodily compliance or establish a health outcome. A chosen pace is adjustable and is not a universally suitable prescription. Free breathing and pausing remain available; complex reasoning does not have to finish within an inhalation or exhalation. No breath holds are required.

The [NHS guidance on breathing exercises for stress](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/) recommends comfortable, gentle breathing without forcing it and presents counting as something people may find helpful. That guidance supports the design's emphasis on comfort and choice. It does not validate FORM, its particular pacing defaults, its training schedule or an intelligence benefit.

## Evidence boundary

The project implements a proposed practice architecture. Successful play can demonstrate improvement on its exercises. Broader transfer, durable cognitive changes, wellbeing effects and IQ changes would require separate research with suitable comparisons and measurements. Neither symbolic stage labels nor a polished interface supply that evidence.
