# Reference notes and boundaries

## Public exercise source

The heart and body exercises are adapted from [Project Meaning](https://o0ooooooooooo0o.github.io/Project-Meaning/), using its [source repository](https://github.com/o0oooooOooooo0o/Project-Meaning) at commit [`fb0553e87b840775438fa3107490b0085b067192`](https://github.com/o0oooooOooooo0o/Project-Meaning/commit/fb0553e87b840775438fa3107490b0085b067192). This adaptation runs locally; it does not embed or fetch the original site at runtime.

The retained structure is:

- **Heart-Coherence:** heart-focused breathing, heart feeling, then deepening. Phase boundaries occur at 33% and 66% of the selected duration. The source duration choices are 15, 30, 60, 120 and 180 minutes.
- **Body-Sensation Mapping:** location, quality, intensity, movement, direction, coherence and meaning-tone. Each receives 30 seconds before the next, with a repeating sequence. The source duration choices are 15, 30 and 60 minutes.
- **Sound:** synthesized bowl cues, a completion-bell sequence and optional ambient pulse settings of 1, 2, 3, 8, 10 or 12 Hz, plus bells without an ambient pad. Sound is off by default in this adaptation. The frequencies are listening preferences; no entrainment, health or intelligence effect is asserted.

The adaptation adds the shape-specific Notice → Recall → Relate → Revise teaching, optional source-preserving notes and reversible self-assessment for both exercises. It makes natural breathing the default and keeps the optional source-style five-second inhalation/five-second exhalation cue. It also makes uncertainty explicit: no sensation, no clear meaning and an inconclusive comparison remain valid observations. Timer lengths are choices, not required or clinically prescribed doses.

## Breathing sources

[Leicestershire Partnership NHS Trust: breathing exercises for stress, anxiety and panic](https://www.leicspart.nhs.uk/mental-health/advice/breathing-exercises-for-stress-anxiety-and-panic/) recommends gentle, comfortable breathing without forcing, optional equal counts up to five, and no pause or breath hold. A count is not necessarily a second. The guidance supports comfort and choice; it does not establish that this application's fixed timing is best for every person.

[Shaffer, Meehan and Zerr (2020), A Practical Guide to Resonance Frequency Assessment for Heart Rate Variability Biofeedback](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2020.570400/full) describes individualized adult resonance assessment around 4.5–6.5 breaths per minute, using actual heart-rate and respiration measurements. Its instructions emphasize easy breathing without excessive depth and stopping if uncomfortable. It discusses differing evidence on inhalation/exhalation ratios rather than establishing one universally optimal ratio.

These sources inform the optional, unforced cue. The application has no heart-rate or respiration sensor and does not conduct resonance assessment. Five seconds per phase produces a six-cycle-per-minute cue; that arithmetic does not establish health benefit, individual suitability or physiological coherence. A person can use their own rhythm, pause or end at any time. More advanced shapes do not change breathing demands.

## Meaning of the shape progression

The fourteen forms name ways to organise observations and relations. They do not classify people or measure IQ. The teaching moves from choosing one feature, preserving its address and comparing episodes toward contextual functions, transfer, competing accounts and evidence-led revision. This is a philosophical curriculum and a design interpretation, not a validated ordering of human capacities.

A return has an identifiable earlier source. N counts deliberately committed observations in a declared shape/exercise stream. The preserved source, a current recollection and an interpretation have different roles. Repetition or a vivid recollection does not verify a memory, an emotion label or another person's intentions. A correspondence between episodes does not by itself establish causation or successful transfer.

Readiness records the learner's judgement about applying a shape in each exercise. Neither that judgement nor a completed timer provides objective evidence of cognitive improvement. The current interface does not score private experience or use the earlier authored-challenge mastery system.

## Evidence boundary

The application's immediate commitments are inspectable teaching instructions, a reusable exercise setting and preserved sources for later comparison. Educational transfer, durable cognitive change, wellbeing effects and clinical outcomes would require separate research with appropriate measurements and comparisons. The exercise source and the breathing references do not validate those claims for Philosophy of Intelligence.
