How to play

All four pieces leave the centre together. Tap a lane or its side button to steer it. Use the arrows to slide along that edge, Rotate to turn clockwise and Drop to land immediately. The dotted outline shows the landing place.

Fill a row at the top or bottom, or a column on the left or right. Each side gets a fresh piece as soon as its last one lands. Keep all four stacks clear of their starting positions.

Keyboard: W/A/S/D selects top/left/bottom/right. Arrow keys slide in screen directions or speed up toward the selected edge. Z/X rotates, Space drops and P pauses.
