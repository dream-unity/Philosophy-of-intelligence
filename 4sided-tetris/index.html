How to play

Four pieces leave the centre together. Each coloured keypad always controls its matching edge. Press several pads together, or hold a slide on one while rotating or dropping another.

The board is always turning. Its direction reverses at random, even while the pieces are paused. Each keypad follows the colour of its starting edge as the board turns.

Fill rows against the top and bottom, and columns against the left and right. Each dotted outline shows where that piece will land. A stack reaching its entrance ends the game.

On a keyboard, the four keys in each row below slide one way, slide the other way, rotate clockwise and drop, in that order.
