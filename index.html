relationship.

A creative philosophy of how understanding takes form.

Experiment with the four lenses ↓

A mind can collect many things and still miss the structure between them. This practice asks a different question: what can you connect, preserve, test and revise?

From a point of contact to an organised whole.

The Circle begins with one selected aspect. A Triangle holds two aspects and their relationship together. A Prism connects whole systems. A Sphere represents flexible understanding that can move between perspectives without losing the source.

The shapes are a teaching language. They describe operations to practise, not fixed types of people.

Depth and memory are different demands.

You can work with a sophisticated relation while keeping the source visible. You can also practise retrieving a simple relation from further back. Philosophy of Intelligence lets those demands develop separately.

A pattern becomes useful when it can do something.

An analogy should preserve roles, expose a meaningful difference and support an inference you can check. A function can be an equation, a conditional rule or a short procedure. Its limits belong alongside it.

The observer belongs inside the model.

When a habit of interpreting becomes visible, it too can be examined. Change one part of the process, inspect the result and return to the world. A useful practice can also know when to simplify.

The optional guide uses a comfortable, adjustable inhale/exhale cycle with no holds. Four seconds in and four seconds out is an editable starting point, not a universal healthy rate. Philosophy of Intelligence does not sense your breath or assess your health. The rhythm never decides whether an answer is correct.
