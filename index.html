THE IDEA BEHIND THE PRACTICE

Intelligence as
relationship.

A manual for noticing, connecting and revising.

Each shape frames the relationships you perceive within a scene. The breath-back action stays complete from the beginning; the number and organisation of selected relationships change.

Begin with a meaningful relationship.

“The ocean contains the boat” is one relationship between two contents. Circle holds that complete relationship. Half-circle holds two distinct meanings: the ocean contains the boat, and the boat enables passage across the ocean. An inverse wording of the same meaning does not add another relationship.

Let the shape organise the scene.

Triangle holds three connected relationships; the polygon sequence grows to eight. A triangular prism joins two triads with three explicit bridges, giving nine relationships. A cube joins two four-link cycles with four bridges, giving twelve. The bridges count only when they are selected meanings within the current scene.

Count structure, not properties.

The working atlas gives the dodecahedron and icosahedron thirty relationships each, arranged differently. The declared geodesic mesh has 120. Sphere integrates that same finite, recoverable network in these examples; it does not stand for remembering infinitely many relationships. These are declared teaching conventions, not measurements of mental capacity.

Receive, encode, then return.

On the inhale, receive and select the current relationships. On the exhale, encode them at the next distinct address in your familiar home before deliberately retrieving the earlier entry. Recover that exact entry, compare, and append any revision. Keep both originals. The memory address and the historical comparison are part of the method at every shape; they do not inflate the scene’s relationship count.

Keep the exercise and the shape distinct.

Consciousness Stream works with imagery, thought and meaning within the mind. Heart-Coherence supplies a setting of attention and care. Body-Sensation Mapping supplies sensation and context. The shape organises conceptual relationships within whichever exercise you choose; a body-mapping dimension is not an extra relationship by itself.

Keep the return exact and the breathing comfortable.

A continuing scene is valid; a new scene is not required on every inhale. Use one entry per actual breath only when the whole action fits comfortably. Otherwise take additional natural breaths and count completed entries. The note form uses this entry-based N-back count. Prepare the larger models before rehearsing them; complexity never requires holds, forced depth or racing through a breath.

The shape atlas is a philosophical practice convention. Personal interpretations remain open to revision. The page does not measure intelligence, breathing or physiological coherence.
