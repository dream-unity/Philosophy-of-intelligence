How can ten names fill six pairs?

Each duo has two rival members. Duties cycle from friend or foe to spy or double agent, then civilian or foreigner, before starting over.

The two duos in the order take the active duty. Read the remaining three duos in number order. The first two take the next duty and the last two take the final duty. The middle duo serves twice.

In each duty, the friend sides with the contact, the spy sides with the next lead, and the civilian sides with the previous lead. Their partners take the opposing roles.

Tap a cutout or its nameplate to fire. On a keyboard, aim with 1–9 or 0, then press Space. A miss costs one bullet.

Your second shot ends the trial. If all remaining members of the assigned pair pass you, you die.
