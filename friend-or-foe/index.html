    How to play

    1. Read your order. It describes two of the ten names. Both can be allies.
    2. Work the evidence. Assign two names to each role in your notebook. You get the answer key after the trial.
    3. Take two shots. Five cutouts start together. The other five enter as lanes open. Tap a cutout or its name to select it. The range closes when you find the pair or all ten have gone by.

    Open Briefing during play to stop the clock and check your notes. On a keyboard, use 1–5 to aim at a lane or use the arrows to move the sight. Space fires and F lets the aimed cutout pass.
